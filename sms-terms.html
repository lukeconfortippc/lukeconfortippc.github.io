Program description

The Cuda Digital SMS program sends customer-service text messages: automated follow-ups when your call to us was missed, replies to inquiries you submit, and occasional requests to review our services after a completed project. Messages are one-to-one service communications, not marketing lists.

Consent / opt-in

You opt in by providing your phone number directly to Cuda Digital in the course of service: by calling our phone line (missed-call follow-ups are sent only to the number that called), by submitting the contact form on this website, or by providing your number when engaging our services.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies; typically 1–4 messages per interaction.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Cuda Digital does not charge for text messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe.

Help / support

Reply HELP to any message, or contact us at luke@cudadigital.com.

Privacy

Your phone number and opt-in information are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
