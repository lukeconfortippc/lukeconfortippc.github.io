Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Cuda Digital ("we," "us," or "our") provides strategic PPC advertising management and customer-communication services. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — including our practices for SMS/text messaging.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide — your name, phone number, email address, and the contents of any inquiry you submit through our website, by phone, or in the course of doing business with us.
  • Service data — records of our communications with you, such as missed-call events, inquiry responses, and review requests.

How we use your information

We use the phone numbers customers voluntarily provide — by calling our phone line, submitting a form on our website, or providing a number when engaging our services — solely to send service-related communications. These include missed-call follow-ups, replies to inquiries, and occasional review requests. Typical volume is 1–4 messages per interaction.

SMS/text messaging privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding the vendors and platform providers necessary to deliver the messages themselves.

Data retention and security

We retain contact information only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Access is restricted to personnel and systems essential for delivering communications.

Your choices

You may stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message; you will receive a single confirmation of your opt-out. Reply HELP for assistance. To ask about, correct, or delete information we hold about you, contact us at the address below.
